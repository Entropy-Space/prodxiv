# Paper benchmarks

Use this component only when reproducible measurements or results exist.

Require:

- the measurement date and environment;
- dataset, workload, and relevant configuration;
- metric definitions and collection method;
- comparison target and version when applicable;
- repetitions, aggregation, and uncertainty;
- source data or reproducibility instructions;
- limitations on what the result establishes.

Tests, benchmark-shaped code, planned experiments, and marketing claims are
not benchmark results. When the requirements are not met, omit the Benchmarks
section and avoid performance claims.
