# Project intent

Use this component to collect product knowledge the repository cannot prove.

Ask a short, adaptive interview covering:

- the original problem and intended users;
- why existing products or approaches were insufficient;
- rejected alternatives and important tradeoffs;
- surprising implementation or adoption lessons;
- known limitations and unresolved decisions;
- the meaning of any measured results.

Label answers as author statements until the author approves their wording.
Preserve uncertainty when the author cannot answer. Do not turn missing intent
into a confident narrative.
